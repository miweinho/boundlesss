# Map Design: The Roots of the Blighted Thicket (Skeleton Cave)

## 1. Lore & Backstory

### The Origin
This area is not a natural formation, but the buried ruins of an ancient Dark Elf sanctuary. Centuries ago, the Spider Queen unleashed a devastating spell that scorched the surface of the sacred forest.

Refusing to abandon their home, the noble Dark Elves retreated into the deep underground root systems to survive. However, the Queen’s corruption and the collapse of the surface trapped them there. They suffocated in the dark, their hearts filled with hatred for the monsters that destroyed their home.

### The Present
The **cave** is now a silent ossuary. The elves have become **Skeletons (Bone Sentinels)**. They are not mindless zombies; they are animated by an "Everlasting Grudge" and "Killing Intent." They remain at their posts, eternal guardians sworn to slaughter any monster that enters their domain.

## 2. The Conflict: Why they fight Morph
To the Skeletons, **Morph** appears only as a Slime, a vile creature born from the very corruption that destroyed their civilization.

* **The Misunderstanding:** They cannot see Morph’s human soul. They see a monster of the Spider Queen.
* **The Reaction:** Their instinct is to purge the "infection." They attack immediately using the archery skills they mastered in life.

## 3. Enemy Profile: Bone Sentinels
Based on the Dark Elf remains found in the Blighted Thicket.

* **Visuals:** Skeletons holding ancient, rotted bows. Some wear tatters of noble elf armor.
* **Combat Behavior:**
    * **Ranged Attacks:** They keep their distance and fire arrows.
* **Passives:**
    * **Fire Resistance:** Having burned to death once, fire no longer harms them.
    * **Strong Bones:** High defense against melee/physical attacks.
* **Weakness:** Vulnerable to Magic or Acid attacks (which Morph may have acquired from the Goblin Alchemist).
* **Morph's Absorption:** Absorbing these enemies grants Morph **Ranged/Projectile capabilities**, allowing him to shoot bone shards or slime spikes.

## 4. Map Boss: Sylas, The First Ranger
**Description:** The corrupted skeleton of the former Captain of the Elf Guard. He stands fused with the petrified roots of a massive tree that blocks the exit gate.

**Lore:** Sylas was the last to fall. He died standing in front of the gate to the deeper forest, swearing that no evil would ever pass him. Even in death, his will keeps the gate sealed.

**Combat Mechanics:**
* **High Defense:** He is heavily armored compared to normal skeletons.
* **Multi-Shot:** Instead of firing one arrow, he fires a "Fan of Arrows" (3 projectiles at once), forcing Morph to dodge carefully.
* **Stationary Threat:** He guards the exit, forcing Morph to use his newly acquired ranged attacks to damage him from safety.

## 5. Level Progression & Transition
* **Entry:** Morph goes into the cave from the Goblin Village above.
* **The Journey:** Morph navigates the claustrophobic **cave**, fighting ranged Skeleton enemies and learning to use projectile attacks.
* **The Climax:** Morph confronts and defeats **Sylas**.
* **The Exit:**
    * Upon absorbing Sylas, Morph assimilates the Captain's authority.
    * The ancient gate, recognizing the energy of its Captain within Morph, unlocks and opens automatically.
    * Morph proceeds to the next region, one step closer to the Spider Queen.
