# **Mobpedia**

## Basic mobs

> **Slime**  
>> Description: Weakest mob in the game
>
>> Use cases: Can be killed, drops xp and can only digest.
>
>> Mastery: Boosts profile absorption success rate per mastery level (after X kills + absorptions = +25%)

> **Goblin**  
>> Description: Weak mob, dangerous as a group
>
>> Use cases: Can be killed, drops xp and stabs/slashes with daggers. Can be absorbed.
>
>> Profile: Stab attack with a chance to bleed enemy; Passive ability - high drop rate/+ crit %?
>
>> Mastery: Boosts crit rate %.

> **Spider**  
>> Description: As a pawn of the spider queen, this mob can be found lurking around in every biome in search for MC. Originally from the cursed/corrupted woods. They are fast attackers with a potentially toxic bite.
>
>> Use cases: Can be killed, drops xp and can be absorbed. Uses bite attacks with a chance of poisoning opponent/leeching vitality/causing a hemorage.
>
>> Profile: Bite(depending on the spider type) with chance of inflicting status ailment; Passive ability - +speed/evasion?
>
>> Mastery: Boosts % rate and damage/duration of status ailment (after X kills + absorptions = +X%)

> **Skeleton**  
>> Description: Undead beings found in the blighted thicket. Born from deceased elves with an everlasting grudge. Long ago this forest was lush, tall, mystical and home to the noble dark elf race. Legends say an unfortunate and horrific tragedy took place that mutated and scorched the entire forest to the ground. The elves, refusing to leave their sacred forest were fully whiped out, leaving only behind their skeletons brimming with killing intent.
These lingering remains plague the blighted thicket with their numbers, and the skills partially inherited from their predecessors.
>
>> Use cases: Can be killed, drops xp and can be absorbed. Uses ranged attacks with bow and arrow. Can leave bleeding status or pierces through opponent.
>
>> Profile: Arrow attack with chance of inflicting bleed or piercing enemies; Passive ability - fire resistance/+ melee defense (strong bones + has no flesh/vital organs. but is manipulated by magic born from the killing intent). Weaker against magical attacks.
>
>> Mastery: Boost defense and ranged damage.
