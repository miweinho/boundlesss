# General Territory

Spider Queen's forest of monsters.

## Verdant Grove

The calmest place in the forest. However, it continues to be a perilous environment for the player despite featuring weaker mobs.

### Mucus Meadow

Description: The starting place for the player and the main habitat for slimes.
Perfect for a tutorial!

Spawns: Slimes (Common), Basic Spider (Uncommon), Basic Goblin (Rare)

### Chief Hut

Description: An area where the biome boss fight occurs. Battle against the Goblin Chief (pseudo-boss). Defeat him to be able to leave the goblin forest. (Might be the boss fight for the presentation)

## Azure Lake

Transitional area between goblin forest and blighted thicket. Common habitat of water elemental beasts.
