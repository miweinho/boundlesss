# Corrupted Forest

>Morph enters the Corrupted Forest, the inner ring of the forest where the Spider Queen’s influence is strongest.
>This zone is a convergence point: corrupted goblins (stronger goblins + I can change the sprite coloration to corrupted), higher-tier spiders, and undead creatures wander in patterns that used to reflect territorial boundaries but have now collapsed into chaos.
>
>The corruption here is arcane rather than natural decay; it spreads through webs, spores, and purple-stained soil. This is a place where the Queen’s magic is actively reshaping the forest, and every creature caught inside is slowly losing its mind or form.
>
>Morph reaches this map knowing that crossing it will bring him to the Spider Queen’s lair.
>This is the final buffer zone between survival and confrontation.



## Choice story

These can be mid-game popups or queues:

> ### Entrance
>
>Morph notices the corruption thickening.
>A purple path marks the way forward, in an environment tinged with arcane residue.
>
>Short popup:
>
>“This part of the forest has a dense corruption than anything I’ve seen… The Queen is close.”

> ### COLLAPSED GOBLIN (optional implementation)
>
>Two goblin corpses are surrounded by webs → one fully corrupted, one partially resisting.
>
>(Branching choice can happen here)



> ### THE CORRUPTED GOBLIN SOUL (CHOICE)
>
> Morph finds the soul of the half-corrupted goblin, a glowing remnant like the ones dropped from mobs (maybe we can make it look special).
>
> The soul speaks in fragmented whispers.
>
> “I… tried to resist… the webs… the Queen’s call…”
>
> The goblin’s soul offers Morph a bargain:
>
>
>
>> #### CHOICE A — “Free the Goblin’s Soul” (Mercy Path)
>>
>>Morph uses his devour ability not to assimilate it, but to break the Queen’s hold and purge it.
>>
>>Narrative Outcome:
>>
>>The soul dissolves peacefully.
>>
>>A faint “thank you” echoes.
>>
>>The ground around the remains briefly loses corruption.
>>
>>**Gameplay Outcome:**
>>
>>Morph receives a temporary blessing:
>>
>>+10–15% absorption success for the entire corrupted forest map (or another buff like a big defense buff against enemies + spider queen).
>>
>>A hidden spider ambush later in the map is weakened (fewer corrupted spiders spawn).
>>
>>**World Logic:**
>>Mercy creates resistance to the Queen’s influence; the forest briefly aligns with Morph.
>
>
>
>> #### CHOICE B — “Consume the Partially Corrupted Soul” (Power Path)
>>
>>Morph absorbs the corrupted soul, gaining a twisted but potent fragment of goblin power.
>>
>>**Narrative Outcome:**
>>
>>The soul screams as it’s pulled in.
>>
>>A faint whisper remains inside Morph.
>>
>>**Gameplay Outcome:**
>>
>>Morph receives a new corrupted buff:
>>
>>+20% damage vs spiders + spider queen but −10% defense (Fragile corruption).
>>
>>The coming spider ambush is harder (extra corrupted spider).
>>
>>**World Logic:**
>>Power accelerates corruption attraction — the forest “notices” him and reacts aggressively.
>
>
>
>> #### CHOICE C — “Leave the Soul Untouched” (Neutral Path)
>>
>>Morph walks away.
>>
>>**Narrative Outcome:**
>>
>>The soul fades naturally.
>>
>>Nothing shifts in the environment.
>>
>>**Gameplay Outcome:**
>>
>>No buff. No penalty.
>>
>>Spider ambush is default difficulty.
>>
>>**World Logic:**
>>Morph avoids interfering; corruption continues without reacting directly to him.



> ### Final ambush (Exit Zone / Lair Entrance)
>
>All three choices lead to the same endpoint, a spider ambush. The ambush difficulty depends on the branch.
>
>If Mercy → weaker ambush
>
>If Power → stronger ambush
>
>If Neutral → standard ambush
>
>As Morph approaches the final web-barrier leading to the Queen:
>
>Popup:
>
>“This is it. No more running.”
>
>And the final corrupted spider(s) drop from the treetops, marking the last challenge before transitioning to the Queen’s Lair scene.
